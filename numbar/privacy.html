Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. Information We Collect

NumBar does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to our servers. All API connections, including Google Analytics OAuth tokens, are stored securely on your local device using macOS Keychain and UserDefaults.

2. Third-Party Services

NumBar connects to third-party services like Google Analytics. By using this integration, you are bound by the Google Terms of Service and Privacy Policy. NumBar only accesses the data necessary to display your requested metrics.

3. Google API Data Usage

NumBar's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Security

We take the security of your data seriously. All sensitive credentials are encrypted and stored locally in the Apple Keychain according to macOS security best practices.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at glvnapps@gmail.com.
