Stocking Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: November 30, 2023

The Stocking application does not collect, store, or transmit any user data or personal financial information to our servers. All portfolio data is saved exclusively on your local device. Therefore, to permanently delete your data, you simply need to remove it from your device using the instructions below.

Android Instructions:

  1. Open Settings.
  2. Scroll down and select Apps.
  3. Tap the See all option at the bottom.
  4. Tap the Stocking app.
  5. Tap Storage and cache.
  6. Tap Clear storage to permanently clear your data and cache.

iOS Instructions:

To permanently delete all your Stocking application data on iOS, you simply need to delete the app from your device. You can do this in two ways:

  • Long-press the Stocking app icon on your Home Screen and select Remove App, then tap Delete App.
  • Navigate to Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Stocking and select Delete App.
